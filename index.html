    About Lunar Mouse Studios

    Lunar Mouse Studios is the home of the Simple Space Universe (S.S.U.), a shared galaxy where each of our games tells another part of the story. Horizons S.S.G. is the first chapter, and its playtest is coming to Steam this month. I.S.G. is next.

    We're Eric and Chris, and it all started with Final Fantasy. We met online and instantly bonded over our Discord icons: Cecil and Kain. We've been buddies ever since, going from MMOs to space simulators to, eventually, making our own games.

    Godot is our engine of choice for our games. Alongside the S.S.U., we're also working on educational games and other projects whenever an idea won't leave us alone.
